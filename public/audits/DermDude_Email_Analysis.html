Critical Issues Identified

Critical · Deliverability

Abandoned Checkout has a 5.44% bounce rate

Anything above 2% is a red flag. At 5.44%, this flow is actively hurting your sender reputation with ISPs. Combined with a 12.64% open rate (well below the ~40% benchmark), this suggests significant list hygiene issues with the audience entering this flow. This is likely dragging down deliverability across ALL your emails.

Critical · Wasted Volume

LP | EDNO is sending 100K emails for $337

This Longplay flow sent nearly 100,000 emails and generated a 3% open rate and 0.01% order rate. It's a pure deliverability drain — every low-engagement send hurts your domain reputation. This flow should be paused immediately and audited.

Critical · Revenue Leak

Post-Purchase: 90K recipients → $2,013

This is your highest-volume flow by far, touching 90,516 people — and it's producing a 0.06% order rate. At industry-average performance (0.5%), this flow alone could generate ~$20K. The content likely isn't incentivizing a next purchase or cross-sell effectively.

Critical · Duplicate Flows

Multiple overlapping abandoned/welcome flows

There are 2+ welcome series, 4+ abandoned cart/checkout variants, and a mix of (GR0), (2020V), and (0BP) tagged flows — many archived or draft. This fragmentation splits traffic, makes optimization impossible, and creates inconsistent customer experiences.

Recommendations — Prioritized by Revenue Impact

Priority 1 · Week 1–2

Pause LP | EDNO immediately, clean Abandoned Checkout list

The EDNO flow is destroying deliverability for $22/month in return. Pause it today. For Abandoned Checkout, add a profile filter to exclude anyone who hasn't opened or clicked in 90+ days, and suppress hard bounces. This protects every other flow in the account.

Expected impact: Improved inbox placement across all flows within 2–4 weeks

Priority 2 · Week 2–3

Rebuild the Post-Purchase flow with cross-sell + review request logic

With 90K recipients, even a small improvement is worth thousands. Structure the flow as: Email 1 (Day 2) = order confirmation + education, Email 2 (Day 5) = product tips/usage, Email 3 (Day 10) = review request, Email 4 (Day 14) = cross-sell complementary product with a time-sensitive offer. Segment by product purchased.

Potential uplift: +$15,000–$20,000/year at 0.3–0.5% order rate

Priority 3 · Week 3–4

Overhaul the Winback flow — it's touching 48K people for 0.13% conversion

The current winback has a 0.24% click rate, which means the offers and messaging aren't resonating. Rebuild with aggressive segmentation: 60-day lapsed (light nudge), 90-day (discount offer), 120-day (last chance). Include a sunset/suppression at 150+ days to protect deliverability.

Potential uplift: +$7,000–$10,000/year at 0.3–0.5% order rate

Priority 4 · Week 4–5

Consolidate Welcome Series into a single, optimized flow

There are currently 3 welcome series — (GR0) Welcome, (GR0) Welcome [Shopify], and the archived (2020V) Welcome. Combined they touch 70K+ recipients. Merge into one flow with conditional splits by acquisition source. The primary welcome has a 1.20% click rate — benchmark is 3–5%. Focus on stronger CTAs, product education, and a clear offer.

Potential uplift: +$5,000–$8,000/year from improved CTR and consolidation

Priority 5 · Week 5–6

Optimize Abandoned Checkout: fix content, add urgency, add SMS

This flow has 24K recipients but only a 0.38% order rate (benchmark: 2–4%). The 12.64% open rate tells us subject lines need work. Add: stronger subject lines with urgency/curiosity, cart contents in the email body, a 10% incentive in email 2 or 3, and ensure SMS is firing as a complement (the SMS version is doing 1.91% order rate — much stronger).

Potential uplift: +$8,000–$15,000/year at 1–2% order rate

Priority 6 · Week 6–8

Launch a Campaign (broadcast) strategy — this is the biggest missing piece

There is zero campaign data in this report, which is a major red flag. Flows handle triggered/automated revenue, but campaigns (weekly sends, promos, content, product launches) typically drive 40–60% of a healthy email program's revenue. DermDude needs a consistent 2–3 campaigns/week cadence targeting engaged segments.

Potential uplift: This is the single biggest lever — could add $3,000–$8,000+/month

Priority 7 · Ongoing

Build out missing flows: Replenishment, VIP, Sunset

For a consumable grooming product brand, a replenishment reminder flow is a no-brainer (trigger at 25/50/75-day post-purchase based on product). A VIP/loyalty flow for repeat buyers (3+ orders) with early access or exclusive offers will boost LTV. A sunset flow to suppress unengaged contacts protects deliverability.

Potential uplift: +$5,000–$10,000/year combined
