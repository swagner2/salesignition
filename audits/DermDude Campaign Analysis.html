Key Findings & Recommendations

CRITICAL Click Rate is Dangerously Low (0.25%)

Open rates are healthy at 43.5%, but clicks average just 0.25% — meaning people open but don't engage with the content. The gap between opens and clicks suggests emails aren't driving action. Fix: Redesign email templates with a single, clear CTA above the fold. Test button-forward layouts vs. current design. Reduce link clutter and focus each email on one action.

CRITICAL Placed Order Rate Declining Year-over-Year

Placed order rate has dropped from 0.08% (2022) to 0.06% (2025) to 0.02% (2026 YTD). Despite sending more campaigns and to larger lists, conversions per email are falling. Revenue per campaign dropped from $501 (2023 peak) to ~$242 (2025-2026). Fix: Prioritize conversion-focused emails over volume. Implement proper A/B testing on offers and landing pages. Review whether the website checkout experience has degraded.

WARNING Over-Sending is Diluting Performance

Campaign volume jumped from ~10/month (2022) to 19+/month currently. More sends haven't translated to more revenue — they've actually diluted it. The 2023 sweet spot was ~25 campaigns/month with $500 avg revenue. Now it's similar volume at $242 avg. Fix: Cut send frequency by 25-30%. Focus on fewer, higher-quality campaigns. Implement a "campaign calendar" that prioritizes quality over quantity.

WARNING List Health Issues — Bounce Rates Spiking

44 campaigns exceeded 1% bounce rate, with several hitting 5-9%. Notably, resends consistently underperform and spike bounces. The Jan 2026 "Holy Foot" campaign hit 6.28% bounce on 19K recipients — that's a deliverability risk. Fix: Implement aggressive list hygiene. Sunset unengaged subscribers after 90 days of inactivity. Stop resending to the full list — only resend to non-openers with a new subject line.

WARNING "Re:" Subject Line Trick — High Revenue but Risky

"Re:" prefixed subjects average $747/campaign vs. $406 without — nearly 2x. But they also have lower open rates (39.8% vs 44.8%), meaning they attract a more purchase-ready audience but annoy others. Overuse will train subscribers to distrust your emails. Fix: Use sparingly (max 1x/quarter) for genuinely important follow-ups, not as a gimmick.

OPPORTUNITY Product Launches & BFCM Drive the Most Revenue

BFCM campaigns average $618/send, product launches average $489, vs. $357 overall. The Strawberry Reminder, Illegal Shave Cream Tease, and Pumpkins BACK campaigns all topped $1,300. Action: Build a product launch cadence — tease → announce → reminder — for every new SKU. This 3-email sequence consistently outperforms one-off blasts.

OPPORTUNITY Smaller, Engaged Lists Outperform Broad Blasts on Efficiency

Broad/Newsletter sends average $523 revenue but go to 30K+ people (low RPR). Engaged segments achieve similar order rates with half the recipients and better engagement metrics. The best RPR campaigns ($0.08-0.10/recipient) were sent to engaged segments of 5K-16K. Action: Default to engaged segments for regular campaigns. Reserve broad sends for major sales events only (BFCM, product launches).

OPPORTUNITY SMS is Underutilized and Click Rate is Declining

SMS generates $26.6K across 201 campaigns but click rates have fallen from 3.6% (2022) to 1.0% (2026). SMS should be a high-conversion channel. Action: Reduce SMS frequency, use only for flash sales and time-sensitive offers. Include exclusive SMS-only deals to train subscribers to click. Coordinate SMS timing with email (SMS 2-4 hours after email, not simultaneously).

OPPORTUNITY Thursday & Sunday Are the Best Send Days

Thursday generates the highest avg revenue ($373) with strong open (44.4%) and order rates (0.06%). Sunday follows at $379 avg revenue. Monday and Wednesday consistently underperform. Action: Shift primary campaign sends to Thursday AM. Use Sunday for lifestyle/story content. Avoid Monday sends unless urgency-driven.
