Why List Owners Are Actively Looking To Promote You?


Years ago, I stumbled into something I didn’t understand at the time. I wrote a tiny post in a small Facebook group about how I got my first 100 subscribers. Out of nowhere the group owner, Noah, messaged me and said, “Turn this into a resource so I can share it with my list.”

His list had 50,000 people.  

Mine had 100.

He sent one email and 1,000 people joined my list. Within a few months they spent over $100,000. At first, I thought I had finally gotten lucky… but that wasn’t the full story.


The truth is big list owners need you.

If someone has a list of 1,000… 10,000… 50,000 subscribers, they have to feed that audience every single week with fresh, relevant, meaningful content. If they fail, their open rates drop, trust erodes, and customers disappear. And creating new content nonstop is brutally hard.

That’s why Oprah needed Dr. Phil. She didn’t make him famous because he was special. She made him famous because her audience needed what he knew.


It’s the same today. List owners crave excellent content from real experts who understand real problems that their followers are suffering from. And you can help them!
