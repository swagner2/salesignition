Deliverability audit & diagnosis

Vurbmoto came to me with a deliverability crisis — inbox placement had dropped to 54%, with emails landing in spam across Gmail and Outlook. Their sending domain had accumulated reputation damage from years of unsegmented blasts to an unclean list. I ran a full audit covering SPF, DKIM, DMARC configuration, blacklist status, and sending reputation analysis.

Klaviyo flow architecture

After restoring deliverability, I rebuilt their entire Klaviyo automation stack from scratch — welcome series, abandoned cart recovery, post-purchase follow-up, and win-back sequences. Each flow was built with proper segmentation gates and send-time optimization to protect the newly restored sender reputation.

Email designs

Every email was designed to match Vurbmoto's high-energy brand aesthetic while maintaining deliverability best practices — optimized image-to-text ratio, clean HTML structure, and mobile-first layouts that render properly across all major email clients.

Results

The combination of deliverability restoration and rebuilt flows drove an additional $155,000 in annual email revenue. Inbox placement jumped from 54% to 95%, and email-attributed revenue became one of Vurbmoto's top-performing channels.
