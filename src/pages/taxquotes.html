TaxQuotes

Email nurture sequence

TaxQuotes connects people with unfiled taxes to licensed tax professionals. I wrote and built a 10-email nurture sequence designed to move cold leads toward a consultation booking — timed around the April tax deadline, and structured inside ActiveCampaign for broad audience applicability.

My responsibilities: Email copywriting, 10-email sequence strategy, ActiveCampaign build, mobile-first landing page design
Reached goal: Consultation booking rate up
+28% increase in bookings
Live link: taxquotes.com

Sequence strategy & flow map

The core challenge was converting cold traffic — people who searched for tax help but weren't ready to commit to a professional. I designed a 10-email sequence with a deliberate psychological arc: urgency in the opener, trust-building through the middle, and a hard deadline push at the end timed to the April filing deadline.

The sequence was structured with conditional branching — if a lead booked a consultation at any point, they exited the nurture and entered a pre-appointment reminder flow. Leads who opened but didn't click received a slightly different cadence than fully disengaged contacts.

Email designs

Email 1 led with urgency — IRS penalty awareness and a clear, empathetic tone. The goal wasn't to scare, but to acknowledge the stress of unfiled taxes and position TaxQuotes as the low-friction path to resolution. Subject line testing showed fear-of-penalty framing outperformed generic "file your taxes" messaging by 3x on open rates.

The final push emails were timed to hit inboxes in the last two weeks before the April deadline. These emails stripped away everything except the CTA — short copy, prominent booking button, and a countdown to the filing deadline. This cluster drove 40% of the total bookings from the entire sequence.

Landing page design

Every email in the sequence drove to a single, mobile-first landing page built for one action: book a free consultation. The page featured a live-call option during business hours and a calendar booking widget for after-hours traffic. Form fields were kept to a minimum — name, phone, and filing situation — to reduce friction for an audience that's already anxious about the process.

Results

The 10-email sequence delivered a +28% increase in consultation bookings compared to the previous single-email approach. The combination of urgency-driven copy, conditional branching, and a streamlined landing page turned a cold traffic source into TaxQuotes' most reliable lead-to-booking channel during tax season.

  • 40% — of all bookings came from the final 2 deadline emails
  • 3x — open rate lift from penalty-framing subject lines vs. generic
  • 10 — emails in the sequence, built in ActiveCampaign with conditional branching
