With the agency, my team does the work for you. With Retention OS, I hand you the strategy, templates, AI tools, and coaching so you (or your marketing person) do the work — at a fraction of the cost. You build the capability in-house and keep it forever.

Who is this NOT for?

Brands that want to fully outsource and never touch their email — that's what the agency is for. Retention OS is for brands that want to own and run their program with senior guidance.

Do I need a marketing person, or can I do it myself?

Either works. Many members are founders doing it themselves; others have a marketing manager we level up to a senior standard. The coaching, templates, and AI tools are built so a small team ships like a big one.

What platforms does this cover?

Primarily Klaviyo for email and SMS, across the full lifecycle — pre-sale, post-sale, and retention. On another platform or considering a migration? We'll cover that on the call.

How fast will I see results?

Most members ship their first improved flows within the first few weeks. The goal across the engagement is to grow email & SMS toward 30–40% of total revenue. Your pace depends on how fast you execute — which is exactly what the coaching keeps on track.

What's the commitment?

The monthly plan is month-to-month — cancel anytime. The 6-month plan is a single upfront payment that saves you $500 and gives you the full window to build the whole engine.
